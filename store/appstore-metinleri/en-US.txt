=== AÇIKLAMA (Description) (2384/4000) ===
Start from nothing, fight your way to the belt. YOL: From Cage to Belt is an MMA fighter game where you fight in the cage with your own hands and every decision outside it shapes your career.

🥊 LIVE FIGHTS
Play every round in real time: jab, cross, kick, slip and guard. Read your opponent's wind-up, slip at the right moment and counter. Shoot for the takedown or sprawl; on the ground, strike from the top, hunt the submission or escape from the bottom. Use your style's special move and your signature technique. Not into reflex games? Switch to "Watch tactics", pick your plan and watch the fight unfold.

🎬 FIGHT NIGHT
Walk down the ramp under the spotlights while the announcer screams your name. Go face to face, hear the bell and watch your knockout again in slow motion.

🏋️ CREATE YOUR FIGHTER, RUN YOUR CAMP
Pick your nationality, weight class and background: boxing, muay thai, wrestling, BJJ, sambo, judo, kyokushin, kickboxing or street. Set your camp length, location and game plan; cut weight, talk trash at the press conference and learn techniques from legendary masters.

🏆 FROM AMATEUR TO WORLD CHAMPION
From the amateur league to regional promotions to the biggest stage in the world. Climb the rankings, win belts, defend your title, enter Grand Prix tournaments, fight in new cities on your world tour and enter the Hall of Fame.

📺 LIFE OUTSIDE THE CAGE
The Cage House reality show, your fan club, your own brand, live streams, podcasts, sponsor haggling, social media crises, family life, nightlife and your own gym. But remember: everything has a price. Protect your brain health and stay sharp in retirement.

⚔️ CHALLENGE YOURSELF
Start on Easy, Normal or Legend difficulty and test yourself in challenge modes like Last Chance, Streets to the Top and Iron Man. Share your score in the Daily Fight, the same for everyone every day.

✨ FEATURES
• Real-time live fights or tactics mode
• Cinematic walkouts, round transitions and KO replays
• Perk tree, signature moves and legendary masters
• Nemesis rivals, dynasty mode and comebacks from retirement
• Shareable fight posters and career cards
• Card album, world map and 111 achievements
• Locker: shorts, gloves, belts and walkout music. No pay-to-win
• 3 career slots, playable offline
• Türkçe, English, Español, Português, Русский, Polski

All promotions, people and brands in the game are fictional.
